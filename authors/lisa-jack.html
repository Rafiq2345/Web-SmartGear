About Lisa

Lisa started SmartGearPick with a simple frustration: most tech advice online is either sponsored, shallow, or written by people who never looked past the spec sheet. She wanted a place where a buyer could get a straight answer — what to buy, what to skip, and why — without wading through marketing.

Her approach is simple: no hype, no paid placements in the rankings, and no guessing. Every recommendation is grounded in verified specifications, benchmark data, and real prices checked before publishing — and corrected when they change. If a device has a real flaw, the guide says so plainly.

SmartGearPick's content is researched and fact-checked by a small editorial team, and Lisa is the named editor responsible for every guide. She does not invent credentials or testing claims she cannot back up — what you read is what was actually checked.

What Lisa Covers

Lisa writes across the categories SmartGearPick is known for:

  • Smartphones — budget picks, flagships, and foldables, compared on what matters day to day.
  • Laptops — student laptops, budget machines, and the MacBook line, with real-world battery and performance notes.
  • Audio — headphones, earbuds, and speakers, judged on sound, ANC, and battery life.
  • Gaming — headsets, mice, keyboards, and accessories that are actually worth the money.
  • Smart Home — speakers, doorbells, and devices that make a home smarter without a subscription trap.
  • Accessories — the chargers, stands, and everyday gear that complete a setup.

How Lisa Works

  • Spec-grounded research: every pick starts from verified manufacturer documentation and standards, not press releases.
  • Benchmark cross-checks: performance claims are checked against standardized industry benchmarks before they make it into a guide.
  • Real price checks: prices are verified against live listings before publishing — and the guide tells you when a price is a deal-site report that can change.
  • Corrections, fast: when a specification, price, or fact is wrong, it gets corrected and the article's date is updated. Readers can report errors any time via the contact page.
