## 1. Testing Claim Audit Rules

### Policy & Mandate
All historical articles must be audited for fabricated or unsupported claims of direct physical testing. 

**Prohibited phrasing (unless verifiable empirical lab data exists):**
- "I tested"
- "We tested"
- "Personally tested"
- "Tested for 30 days"
- "Tested 10+ products" / "Tested 15+ products"
- "Real-world testing"
- "Hands-on testing"
- "Lab tested"
- "After weeks of testing"
- "We bought these products"

**Approved transparent replacement phrasing:**
- *"Based on manufacturer specifications and published technical documentation"*
- *"Based on independent expert testing and aggregate benchmark data"*
- *"Based on extensive research from trusted industry sources and verified user feedback"*
- *"According to published benchmark data and technical tear-downs"*
- *"Based on specifications, expert reviews, and available performance metrics"*

## 2. Classification Criteria

| Action | When to Use | Migration Guideline |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Keep** | Content is high quality, topic remains relevant, search intent is satisfied, claims are transparent, and structure is sound. | Retain topic and structure; apply modern template styling and transparent disclosure. |
| **Rewrite** | Topic is valuable and has search potential, but content is thin, overly generic, uses AI cliches, lacks product explanations, or contains unsupported testing claims. | Preserve the topic and URL slug; rewrite from the ground up using the research-backed evaluation framework. |
| **Merge** | Two or more posts compete for the exact same keyword or split search intent across shallow pages. | Consolidate the best insights into a single comprehensive guide; redirect secondary URLs to the primary page. |
| **Remove** | Article has zero search volume, topic is completely obsolete/discontinued, content is duplicate, or cannot reasonably be improved. | Deprecate page; return HTTP 410 (Gone) or 301 redirect to the closest category parent. |
