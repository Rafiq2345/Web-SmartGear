2. Windows 11 vs. ChromeOS on Cheap Hardware

  • Choose ChromeOS (Chromebooks): If your daily tasks happen 100% inside Google Chrome, Google Docs, Canvas, or web apps. ChromeOS runs smoothly on low-power hardware with zero background bloat.
  • Choose Windows 11 (Intel N100): If you need to install standard desktop `.exe` applications, Microsoft Office desktop suites, or iTunes. Always ensure you get an **8GB RAM** configuration.

3. Sub-$300 Traps: 3 Specs You Must Avoid

  1. Dual-Core Celeron Processors (N4020 / N4500): Outdated dual-core chips that stutter during 1080p video. Insist on Quad-Core Intel N100 or Kompanio 500+ chips.
  2. 4GB RAM on Windows: Windows 11 requires nearly 3.5GB just for system background processes. Look for 8GB.
  3. 32GB eMMC Storage: Fills up immediately after Windows updates. Ensure a minimum of 64GB (ChromeOS) or 128GB (Windows).
