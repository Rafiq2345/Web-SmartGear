2. Traditional PIR vs. mmWave Radar Presence Detection

Standard **Passive Infrared (PIR)** sensors only detect moving thermal signatures; as soon as a person remains motionless, PIR sensors register an empty room. **Millimeter-Wave (mmWave) Radar** transmits high-frequency electromagnetic waves that reflect off micro-movements like chest expansion during respiration, providing flawless true occupancy automation.

3. Why Local Automation Architecture Matters

Cloud-dependent smart accessories introduce noticeable 1–3 second latency and fail completely during internet service outages. By combining Matter-over-Thread with local hub execution, your smart home automations trigger instantaneously and function without interruption.
