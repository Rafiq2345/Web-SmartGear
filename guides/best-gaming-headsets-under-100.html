2. Stereo vs. 7.1 Spatial Audio for Competitive Gaming

In tactical first-person shooters like CS2, Valorant, and Rainbow Six Siege, pure high-resolution **stereo with game-engine binaural audio** is generally superior to software virtual surround filters, which can add artificial reverb and obscure distance cues. Look for headsets with clean driver acoustic separation.

3. Microphone Quality: What Matters on Discord

A quality gaming headset microphone requires two things: a cardioid pickup pattern (rejecting mechanical keyboard clicks behind the capsule) and a foam or mesh pop filter to disperse vocal plosives.
