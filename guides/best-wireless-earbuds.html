2. Bluetooth Codecs Explained: LDAC vs. AAC vs. LC3

  • AAC (Advanced Audio Coding): The universal standard for Apple iOS devices. Maxes out at 256–320 kbps but features excellent power efficiency.
  • LDAC (Sony High-Resolution): Transmits up to 990 kbps at 24-bit/96kHz on Android devices, delivering three times the data bandwidth of standard Bluetooth.
  • LC3 (Bluetooth LE Audio): The next-generation Bluetooth audio standard offering lower latency and Auracast broadcast audio sharing in public spaces.

3. Passive Noise Isolation & Eartip Sizing

Active noise cancellation is only as effective as the physical seal created by your eartip. Always test multiple silicone or memory foam tip sizes; an improper seal allows low-frequency bass energy to leak out and high-frequency ambient noise to bleed in.
