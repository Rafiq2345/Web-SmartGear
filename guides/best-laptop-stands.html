Why Laptop Ergonomics Matter

When a laptop rests flat on a desk, users consistently tilt their heads forward by 30 to 45 degrees, placing up to 40 pounds of additional gravitational strain on the cervical spine. Elevating the top third of the laptop screen to eye level eliminates neck fatigue and encourages upright sitting posture.
