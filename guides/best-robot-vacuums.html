2. AI RGB Cameras & Structured Light Obstacle Avoidance

Basic LiDAR robots bump blindly into phone charging cables, socks, and pet waste. Premium models integrate **AI RGB camera neural networks** with 3D structured light lasers, identifying and navigating safely around loose wires and sudden obstacles in total darkness without human intervention.

3. Sonic Vibration vs. Dual Spinning Mopping Systems

  • Sonic Vibration (VibraRise): Scrubs up to 4,000 times per minute, breaking down dried juice and muddy paw prints on tile and hardwood.
  • Dual Spinning Discs (180 RPM): Uses downward physical pressure to polish glossy floors and lift stuck-on kitchen grease.
