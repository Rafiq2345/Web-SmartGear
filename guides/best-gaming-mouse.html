2. Polling Rates Explained: 1000Hz vs 4000Hz vs 8000Hz

Standard gaming mice report their position to your PC 1,000 times per second (1,000Hz = 1.0ms reporting interval). Modern 4K and 8K polling rates increase this frequency:

  • 4,000Hz: Reports every 0.25ms, delivering smoother cursor motion on 240Hz+ gaming monitors.
  • 8,000Hz: Reports every 0.125ms, achieving the lowest possible input delay, but requires a fast modern CPU to handle increased USB interrupt traffic.

3. Why Optical Switches Eliminate Double-Clicks

Traditional mechanical mouse switches rely on metal contact leaves that suffer from oxidation over time, requiring "debounce delay" filters that eventually fail and cause accidental double-clicks.

Optical switches actuate via an infrared light beam that breaks when the switch is depressed. Because there are no physical metal contacts to wear out, optical switches are physically immune to double-click failure.
