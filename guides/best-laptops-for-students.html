Key Selection Criteria for College Laptops

College students need laptops that survive long days on campus without requiring frequent power outlet searches. When making your selection, prioritize:

  • Weight & Portability: Keep total carry weight under 3.3 lbs (1.5 kg) to avoid shoulder strain between distant campus buildings.
  • Minimum Unified RAM: Avoid 8GB RAM machines in 2026. Opt for 16GB so research papers with dozens of PDF tabs and lecture recordings do not stutter.
  • USB-C Charging: Ensure the laptop charges via USB-C Power Delivery, allowing you to top up using a portable USB-PD power bank in lecture halls.
