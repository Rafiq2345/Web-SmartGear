Understanding Fast-Charging Protocols in 2026

Modern power banks are defined by their USB-PD (Power Delivery) wattage and PPS (Programmable Power Supply) compatibility:

  • Smartphone Fast Charging (20W – 45W): iPhones charge at peak rates between 20W to 27W, while Samsung Galaxy devices require PPS support for 45W Super Fast Charging 2.0.
  • Laptop Fast Charging (65W – 140W): Ultraportables like the MacBook Air M5 require a minimum of 45W–65W to charge rapidly under active workload.
  • TSA Airline Limit (100Wh): Batteries exceeding 100 Watt-hours (approximately 27,000mAh at 3.7V) require airline pre-approval. All picks below fall safely under the 100Wh threshold for domestic and international carry-on luggage.
