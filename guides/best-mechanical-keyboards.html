Mechanical Keyboard Switch Types Explained

Choosing the right switch type defines your typing comfort and gaming responsiveness:

  • Linear (Red/Yellow): Smooth, consistent keystroke from top to bottom with zero tactile bump. Preferred by competitive FPS gamers for rapid double-tapping.
  • Tactile (Brown/Panda): Features a subtle tactile bump at the actuation point. Ideal for programmers, writers, and hybrid work environments.
  • Magnetic / Hall Effect: Uses magnetic sensors rather than physical metal leaf contacts. Allows adjustable rapid trigger actuation points down to 0.1mm.
