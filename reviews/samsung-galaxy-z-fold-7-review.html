Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 7 Review (2026) — Is It Worth $1,799?

Samsung's flagship foldable delivers a wider 22:9 cover screen, an ultra-thin teardrop hinge that minimizes the center crease, Snapdragon 8 Gen 4 performance, and full desktop Samsung DeX support.

1. Hinge Durability & Crease Visibility

The Galaxy Z Fold 7 refines Samsung's dual-rail teardrop hinge mechanism, allowing the flexible inner Ultra-Thin Glass (UTG) to curve smoothly inside the chassis with zero gap when folded shut. Under direct light, the central display crease is nearly invisible and barely perceptible to the fingertip during scrolling.

2. One UI Multitasking & S Pen Productivity

Samsung's One UI software remains a leading benchmark for foldable productivity. You can run three full applications side-by-side simultaneously (e.g., Google Sheets, Slack, and Chrome), drag and drop images directly between windows, and write handwritten notes with the optional S Pen Fold Edition.

3. Triple Camera System & FlexCam Shooting

Equipped with a 50MP primary sensor with hardware Optical Image Stabilization (OIS), a 12MP ultra-wide, and a 10MP 3x optical telephoto lens, the Fold 7 captures sharp HDR photos. Its folding chassis allows **FlexCam mode**, acting as its own tripod for hands-free group photos and 4K video recording.

4. Battery Life & Daily Ergonomics

The 4,400mAh dual-cell battery delivers approximately 7.5 hours of active screen-on time when alternating between the outer cover screen and large inner tablet display.

5. Pros, Cons & Final Verdict

Pros

  • Unrivaled 3-app split-screen multitasking and Samsung DeX desktop mode
  • Wider 22:9 cover screen feels natural for one-handed typing
  • Ultra-bright 2,600-nit Dynamic AMOLED 2X displays
  • 7 years of guaranteed Android OS and security upgrades

Cons

  • High $1,799 starting MSRP
  • S Pen does not slot inside the phone body (requires special case)

Overall Rating: 9.2 / 10

The definitive productivity powerhouse for mobile professionals who want a phone and tablet in one pocket.
