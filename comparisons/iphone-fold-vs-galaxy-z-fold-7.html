1. Hinge Engineering & Inner Screen Crease

Samsung's dual-rail hinge folds the Z Fold 7 down to a slim closed profile, while Apple uses a precision hinge in a grade-5 titanium frame for the iPhone Duo. Apple's 7.6-inch inner display uses a 10-layer construction with a nano-texture finish designed to reduce glare and make the fold less visible, with an under-display FaceTime camera keeping the viewing surface uninterrupted.

2. Multitasking Software: One UI vs. iOS 27

The true value of a book-style foldable lies in its tablet-sized interior workspace:

  • Samsung One UI: Offers the most mature power-user features: running three apps simultaneously in split-screen, drag-and-drop text/photos across windows, floating pop-up apps, and full desktop PC mode via Samsung DeX.
  • iPhone Duo (iOS 27): Brings native Split View, dual-window instances of the same app, and an expanded StandBy mode that works across both displays, with Apple Pencil (USB-C) support.

3. Camera Telephoto & Sensor Hardware

Foldable chassis constraints require thinner camera modules. The Galaxy Z Fold 7 leads on raw resolution with a 200MP main sensor plus a dedicated 3x telephoto, while the iPhone Duo uses a 48MP Dual Fusion system (Main + Ultra Wide) with a 12MP Center Stage front camera and an under-display FaceTime camera on the inner screen.

4. Which Foldable Ecosystem Fits Your Workflow?

If you depend on complex spreadsheet multitasking and desktop DeX, the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 7 remains the reigning champion. If you prioritize Apple ecosystem continuity — AirDrop, Apple Watch synergy, and ProMotion color calibration — the iPhone Duo brings the foldable form factor to iOS for the first time, starting at $1,999.
